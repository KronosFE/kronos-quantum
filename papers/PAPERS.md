# The Kronos quantum paper series

These codes accompany a peer-reviewable series on quantum computing for fusion. The papers are the
"why and how" in depth; the codes here are the runnable "here it is." The published versions live in
the Kronos publications hub and the Kronos Zenodo community (DOIs listed there); this page indexes them
by title.

| # | Title | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| **2.90** | Quantum Computing | the overview: what quantum computing can and cannot do for fusion, and when |
| **2.91** | Quantum Optimization & Quantum Reinforcement Learning | combinatorial design search; QAOA-class methods (see `KQOPT`) |
| **2.92** | Quantum Machine Learning, Sensing & FTQC | quantum kernels, quantum metrology, and the fault-tolerant horizon (see `KQERN`, `KSENSE`, `KQROSS`) |
| **2.93** | Quantum Algorithms & Control | algorithmic primitives and their control-loop relevance |
| **2.94** | The Burner Quantum Stack | how the quantum layer maps onto the burner |
| **2.95** | ADAPT-VQE & Quantum Chemistry | the chemistry route in depth (see `KQUBIT`, `KMIT`) |
| **1.5** | AI + Quantum Control | the quantum layer inside the AI-native control architecture |

**Where to find them:** the Kronos publications page and the Kronos Zenodo community carry the current,
citable versions with their DOIs. This repository intentionally links rather than re-hosts them, so the
citable record stays single-sourced.

**Honest framing, shared with the papers:** no quantum advantage for fusion is claimed this decade; a
meaningful role is a post-~2036 prospect. What is real today is the tooling and the testing — which is
what the codes in this repository demonstrate.
